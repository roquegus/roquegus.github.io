Calle Nueve Brand Kit

Everything needed to open the social media profiles and keep them looking like one brand: the mark, the colors, the fonts, the voice, the bios, and the images already cut to each platform's size.

Files live at callenueve.com/brand/. Tap any download link on your phone to save the image, then upload it in the app.

The short answer

Start three: Instagram first, then TikTok, then a Facebook Page. Instagram is where souvenir shops, gift buyers and Miami people will find a deck like this, and its grid is the portfolio. TikTok is where a 15-second table video of a chucho moment can travel. The Facebook Page exists so Instagram can be a business account linked to it, so you can post in Cuban and Miami groups, and so the older audience that plays dominoes finds you.

Skip for now: Pinterest (add it in month two, it is good for gift searches and costs nothing), YouTube (post the same short videos later), X and LinkedIn (only if you want them; the banners are here anyway).

To open all three you need: the handle, a business email, your phone for verification, the profile picture, the bio, the link, and nine posts ready before you announce anything. All of that is below.

The mark

A domino tile that reads C9

The mark is a domino tile with "C" above the line and "9" below it. It is the deck itself, and it works at 40 pixels. Use it teal on cream, cream on teal, or cream on ink. Never stretch it, never rotate it, never put it on a photo without a solid plaque behind it. The line across the tile is teal by default and pink for a playful moment.

Rule of thumb for a post: one teal, one cream, one accent. Black pips on cream tiles are the product; let them be the darkest thing in the frame.

Type

Two fonts, one job each

  • Bebas Neue for anything that shouts: names, headlines, numbers, stickers. Always all caps, always with some letter spacing. This is the card index font, so it ties posts to the product.
  • Lato for anything that explains: captions, bios, the website body. Playfair Display is on the website for big serif headlines; on social keep it to Bebas and Lato so posts look like one hand made them.
  • Sacramento (script) only for a word like "cafecito" or "chucho" as a flourish, never for a whole line.

All three are free on Google Fonts and are in Canva's font list, so you can make posts on your phone that match.

Voice

Sounds like a Miami table, not a brand

  • Short and warm. Sentences you would say across the table. Spanglish is welcome. No corporate words.
  • Own the vocabulary: La Gorda, chucho, capicúa, cafecito, la ventanita, Calle Ocho, doble nueve. Explain a term once in the caption, then use it freely.
  • Lines that are already yours: "Same table. Same chucho. No tiles." / "The Cuban double-nine, as a card deck." / "Hecho con amor en Miami." / "La Gorda opens."
  • Never: emojis in the logo, "premium quality" language, stock photos of dominoes that are not yours.

Before you open anything

The checklist

  1. Email: hola@callenueve.com. If that mailbox does not exist yet, create it first (Cloudflare Email Routing to your Gmail is free and takes ten minutes; say the word and it gets set up). Every account should use it, not your personal Gmail.
  2. Phone: your cell, for the verification codes. Turn on two-factor login on each account the day you create it.
  3. Handle check: search @callenueve in Instagram, TikTok and Facebook the same evening.
  4. Profile picture: profile-1024.png, same on all three.
  5. Bio and link: from the section above. The link is callenueve.com until the Stripe buy button is live; then it stays callenueve.com.
  6. Nine posts ready before you tell anyone. An empty grid loses the visitor. The nine are planned below.
  7. Product on hand: one printed deck and the box for photos and videos. Everything else is renders from the Studio.
Instagram, step by step

Open it in fifteen minutes

  1. Open the Instagram app. On your profile, tap your username at the top, then "Add Instagram account", then "Create new account".
  2. Username: callenueve. Email: hola@callenueve.com. Set a password and save it in your password manager.
  3. Tap "Edit profile". Name: Calle Nueve · Domino Cards. Bio: paste the Instagram bio above. Link: callenueve.com. Upload profile-1024.png.
  4. Settings, then "Account type and tools", then "Switch to professional account", choose Business, category "Product/Service". Add the email and skip the phone display.
  5. Post the nine planned posts over three days (three a day) so the grid fills before you announce.
  6. Add four Highlights with the covers here: Rules, Decks, Shop, 305.

TikTok: same email and handle, create the account, then in Settings choose "Switch to Business Account" so you get the website link. Facebook: create the Page from your personal profile (Menu, Pages, Create), then in Instagram settings link the Page under "Accounts Center".
