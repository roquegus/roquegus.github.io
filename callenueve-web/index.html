Questions before you play?

Quick Answers

Is this a different game from dominoes?

No. It is the double-nine domino system translated into a card format. The goal is portability without losing the core play pattern.

How many cards are in the deck?

55 cards, covering every combination from 0-0 through 9-9.

Why cards instead of tiles?

Tiles are great at the table. Cards travel better. Calle Nueve is made for beaches, parks, cafes, flights, and quick games anywhere.

Is this made for collectors or players?

Both. The deck is meant to look like a keepsake and still function as a playable game.
