How it works

Four steps, about two weeks

Send your logo

Fill in the form below. A vector logo (SVG, AI, PDF) prints sharpest, but a good PNG works.

We design it

Within two business days you get a private proof link with the back, all 55 faces and the box.

You approve

One click on the proof. Want a different color on the bar? Say so and we send it again.

We print and ship

Printed on linen-finish stock in a matching tuck box. Ten decks or a thousand.

What is in every deck

Not a promo item. A game.

  • 55 playing cards, 0-0 through 9-9, domino size (1.75 by 3.5 in).
  • Your logo on the back, upright or sideways, and your color on the bar.
  • A rules card with a QR code, so anyone who finds it learns the game.
  • Two chucho cards of Cuban table talk, in Spanish with the English.
  • A tuck box with your name on the front and your story on the back.
  • Premium linen-finish cardstock. Designed and proofed in Miami.

Also for weddings and quinces (the couple's names on the back), events and conferences, restaurants and cafecito windows, and souvenir shops. Shops: see the wholesale line sheet.
