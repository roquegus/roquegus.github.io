The Miami souvenir that gets played, not shelved.

Calle Nueve is the Cuban double-nine domino game as a 55-card deck. Same rules, same strategy, same chucho, in a pocket-size tuck box. Four Miami designs, each with a bilingual box, a rules card with a QR code, and two cards of Cuban table talk. Designed and proofed in Miami.

The four decks

C9-DP · Little Havana

Domino Park

Cream tiles, tobacco-brown Cuban icons for pips (cafecito, maracas, palms, cigars), a terracotta bar, and a Cuban floor-tile back. Cigar-label box with a rooster stamp.

C9-DB · South Beach

Deco Beach

White tiles, flat navy dots, a flamingo-pink bar, and an Art Deco sunburst back with a porthole medallion. Sand-colored box with a lifeguard tower stamp.

C9-MS · Miami

Miami Sunset

Cream tiles with plum dots and a hot-pink bar. The back is a teal-to-pink sunset with a banded sun and palm silhouettes. Sand box with a flamingo stamp.

C9-FC · Miami

Flamingo Card

Drawn like a 1910s tobacco trading card: cream border, teal field, gold reeds, one flamingo, "Miami · Florida" underneath. Cream tiles with deep-teal dots and a gold bar.
