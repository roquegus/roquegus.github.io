# Postcard art for the souvenir decks

Linen postcards, about 1930 to 1945, from the Tichnor Brothers collection at the Boston Public Library
(Digital Commonwealth), marked "no known copyright restrictions". Free to use commercially.

| Letter | Card | Source item | Used on |
|---|---|---|---|
| B | Coral Flamingo, Rare Bird Farm, Miami, Fla. | commonwealth:6108vd846 | Flamingo Card: card back (`B-back.jpg`), box front (`B-box.jpg`) |
| I | "A Miami Beach Sundae", Florida | commonwealth:6108vh78p | Deco Beach: card back (`I-back.jpg`), box front (`I-box.jpg`). The red "OFFICE FILE" proof stamp was inpainted out (OpenCV Telea). |
| L | Greetings from Miami, Florida | commonwealth:6108vc78v | Miami Sunset: card back turned 90 degrees (`L-back.jpg`, reads with the card held sideways), box front on the box blue (`L-box.jpg`) |

Sizes: card backs 597 x 1122 (full bleed), box pictures 489 x 680 (the frame in the Simple box style).
Source scans were fetched at 2400 px through the c9-fetch Vercel build (IIIF `full/!2400,2400/0/default.jpg`).
The `*-source-*.jpg` files are the trimmed, cleaned scans before cropping, for re-cropping later.

`L-box-wide.jpg` (938 x 599) is the whole Greetings postcard for the Retail box, which shows wide pictures uncropped. The handwritten "65213" and printed "543" in the top right corner were inpainted out of `L-source-greetings-from-miami.jpg` (OpenCV Telea plus a little grain).
