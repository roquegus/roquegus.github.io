# Retail kit

Print files for selling to shops. Source HTML plus the files it uses in `src/`; `node render.cjs` (Playwright, Chromium headless shell) writes the PDFs and PNG previews.

| File | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `counter-sign.pdf` | 4 x 6 in portrait, 1/8 in bleed (page 4.25 x 6.25) | Goes in a clear 4 x 6 acrylic sign holder next to the decks. QR opens callenueve.com/play?utm_source=counter. |
| `wholesale-card.pdf` | 6 x 4 in postcard, two sides, 1/8 in bleed | Leave with the shop owner. Prices and terms match callenueve.com/wholesale; change both together. QR opens /wholesale?utm_source=leavebehind. |

Print either at any local print shop or an online postcard printer: 14 pt or 16 pt card stock, matte, full bleed. The counter display itself (a tray or stand for 6 to 12 decks) is bought, not printed: a clear acrylic card-deck display or a small tiered riser.
