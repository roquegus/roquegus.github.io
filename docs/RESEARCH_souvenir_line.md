# Miami souvenir deck line: research summary

Date: 2026-09-21. Full brief with mockups was published as a Claude artifact titled "Miami Souvenir Decks" (link in SESSIONS.md, session 7). This file is the short version so a future session can build without re-researching.

## Goal

Two to four Miami-themed decks sold in souvenir shops and museum stores at $20 to $25. Buyers are visitors who came to see Miami and Cuban culture and want a keepsake.

## Market facts used

- Miami-Dade had 28.2M visitors in 2025, 6.4M international. Top international markets: Colombia, Brazil, UK, Canada, Mexico, Argentina, Spain, Italy, Germany, France. Box copy should be bilingual (English and Spanish).
- On Calle Ocho tourists buy domino sets, cigars, guayaberas, fedoras, roosters, magnets and tees. The Little Havana Visitor Center (next to Domino Park) sells dominoes at the counter.
- Price anchor: Bene Casa Cuban flag double-nine wooden set is $24.99. theory11 premium poker decks retail $12 to $13.
- Gift shops expect keystone (buy at half of retail). Museum buyers want a local story, sustainable packaging (no plastic window, no shrink film), UPC barcodes, low minimums, a one-page line sheet.
- Packaging guidance: a plain tuck box reads "under $15." Above that, add foil, embossing or matte soft-touch. MPC offers gold or silver foil, embossing, holographic, and matte aqueous coating on tuck boxes.
- Cuban dominoes are double nine (55). Dominicans and Puerto Ricans mostly play double six. The same deck plays double six by removing 7s, 8s and 9s. Say this on the rules card.

Rules every back follows: point-symmetric (reads either way up), one pattern plus one border plus one medallion, pattern into the bleed but marks inside the 52 px line, back dark or saturated so it is unmistakable from the white faces, CMYK-safe colors (no true neon), brand mark small inside the medallion.

## Box panels

- Front: deck name, one mark, "Miami", foil on the name.
- Back: 40-word story, three lines of how to play, QR to callenueve.com/play, "Designed in Miami by Calle Nueve".
- Sides: "55 cards, double nine, 2 to 4 players" in English and Spanish, small stamp.
- Bottom: UPC, C9 mark, edition name.
- Matte aqueous on every edition. No plastic window or shrink wrap; paper belly band or sticker seal. Six-deck kraft counter display.

## Money

Retail $24.99. Direct sales net about $23.50. Consignment 60/40 nets $15. Keystone wholesale nets $12.50. For 40 percent margin the deck plus box must cost at most about $7 for wholesale, $7.50 for consignment, $10 direct. Compare with the last MPC invoice; if above $7, the souvenir line needs a bulk order or a second printer quote at 500 plus. Start with consignment at the Little Havana Visitor Center and the Art Deco Welcome Center plus direct sales. Get GS1 UPC codes before pitching museums. Register on Faire.

## Recommendation

Build Domino Park and Deco Beach first. Vice Nights third. Gran Habana as the premium edition once the first two sell. Avoid naming a deck "Calle Ocho" (festival trademark risk).
