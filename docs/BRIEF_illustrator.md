## Fiverr post (copy from here)

**Title:** Vintage Florida travel-poster illustration for a Miami souvenir card deck (card back + box)

**Category:** Graphics & Design > Illustration (or Packaging & Label Design)

**Description:**

I make Calle Nueve, a Cuban double-nine domino game printed as a 55-card deck (callenueve.com). I am launching a Miami souvenir edition for gift shops on Calle Ocho and South Beach and I need original artwork for one deck: the card back and the front of the tuck box.

**The look:** 1930s to 1950s Florida travel poster and linen postcard. Flat color, screen-print feel, limited palette (4 to 6 colors), bold shapes, a little paper texture is fine. Think WPA travel posters, Tichnor Brothers linen postcards, vintage cigar-box labels. Not photoreal, not 3D, not modern flat-vector clip art, no gradients as the main effect, no AI-generated art.

**Subject (pick one deck; I may order the other three later):**
- Domino Park (Little Havana): the domino tables under the pergola at Máximo Gómez Park, a rooster, a cafecito, Cuban floor tile pattern.
- Deco Beach (South Beach): a lifeguard tower on the sand, Art Deco hotel facades, a sunburst sky.
- Miami Sunset: palms against a pink-and-teal sunset over the water.
- Flamingo Card: one flamingo, drawn like a 1910s tobacco trading card, cream border, caption "Miami · Florida".

**Deliverables:**
1. Card back illustration: 1.75 x 3.5 in plus bleed, delivered at 597 x 1122 px at 300 dpi (exact pixel size, it goes straight into my print tool). Must read the same when the card is turned 180 degrees (rotationally symmetric, or a centered subject that does not care which way is up). Nothing important within 72 px of the edge.
2. Tuck box front illustration: 1.83 x 3.57 in, delivered at 549 x 1070 px at 300 dpi. Can be the same art cropped or a companion piece. Leave a clear area for a title band (I add the text).
3. A square spot illustration (the rooster, tower, palm or flamingo alone) on a transparent background, 1200 x 1200 px PNG, for the box back and the label.
4. Source files: layered PSD or AI/SVG, plus flattened PNGs. Fonts are not needed; I set all type myself.
5. Full commercial rights, all uses, worldwide, no attribution. Please price that in.

**Palette (you can shift shades, keep the family):** teal 0D9488, deep teal 0B6F66, pink F472B6, yellow EAB308, gold D97706, tobacco 92400E, ink 2A1610, cream F6EFDF.

**Process:** one rough sketch for approval, one color comp, then final. I answer within a day. Please send two or three examples of poster-style or retro travel work when you reply, that is what I choose on.

**Timeline:** two weeks for the first deck.

**Budget:** [set it: $150 to $400 per deck is the going range for this scope with full rights]

## Spec sheet to send after hiring

**Card back.** Canvas 597 x 1122 px, 300 dpi, RGB (sRGB), PNG or layered source. The outer 36 px on every side is trimmed off (bleed); art must extend to the edge. Keep anything you care about inside the 72 px safe zone (a 453 x 978 px window in the middle). The back must be point-symmetric or a centered subject: when the card is turned upside down at the table it must look the same, because players can see the backs of everyone's cards. A one-way picture (like the flamingo card) is allowed only for the Flamingo Card deck.

**Tuck box front.** 549 x 1070 px at 300 dpi. The whole panel is visible; keep 30 px clear of every edge. Reserve a horizontal band about 549 x 200 px (top or middle, your call) where I place the title "CALLE NUEVE" and a subtitle; leave that band as a flat area or a plain cartouche.

**Spot illustration.** 1200 x 1200 px, transparent PNG plus the vector or layered source. Single subject, no background, reads at 1 in and at 0.4 in.

**Color.** RGB is fine; the printer converts. Avoid neon values that do not print (pure 00FF00 greens, pure magenta). Fine lines thinner than 2 px at 300 dpi will disappear.

**Do not include** any text, logos or the words Calle Nueve; I set the type. No real hotel names, no trademarked characters, no traced stock or AI output.
