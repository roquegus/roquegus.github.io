## Fiverr post (copy from here)

**Title:** Miami illustration for a card deck: card back and box front. Fun, colorful, your take on Miami.

**Description:**

I need original art for a deck of playing cards: the back of the card and the front of the box.

Draw Miami. Your Miami. Fun colors, fun vibes, the version a tourist wants to take home. No specific style required. Things that say Miami: pink and teal, palms, flamingos, Art Deco, the beach, cafecito, neon, the ocean. Use what you like, skip the rest. Surprise me.

Not looking for: photo-real, 3D, clip art, stock vectors, AI art, or any text in the artwork.

Deliverables:
1. Card back, 597 x 1122 px at 300 dpi. Must look right upside down (symmetric, or a centered subject). Keep important details 72 px from the edges.
2. Box front, 549 x 1070 px at 300 dpi. Same art cropped or a companion piece, with a clear band for a title.
3. One element from the design (flamingo, palm, whatever you choose) alone on a transparent background, 1200 x 1200 px PNG.
4. Layered source (PSD or AI) plus PNGs. Full commercial rights, no attribution.

Process: rough sketch, color comp, final. I reply within a day. Please send two or three samples of colorful, fun work with your reply.

Timeline: two weeks. Budget: $150 to $400 with full rights.

## Spec sheet to send after hiring

**Card back.** Canvas 597 x 1122 px, 300 dpi, RGB (sRGB), PNG plus the layered source. The outer 36 px on every side is trimmed off (bleed); art must run to the edge. Keep anything you care about inside the 72 px safe zone (a 453 x 978 px window in the middle). The back must look right upside down: symmetric top to bottom, or a centered subject. A one-way picture is acceptable only if we agree on it first.

**Box front.** 549 x 1070 px at 300 dpi. The whole panel is visible; keep 30 px clear of every edge. Reserve a band about 549 x 200 px (top or middle) as a flat area or plain cartouche where I place the title and a subtitle.

**Spot illustration.** 1200 x 1200 px, transparent PNG plus the vector or layered source. Single subject, no background, must read at 1 in and at 0.4 in.

**Color.** RGB is fine; the printer converts. Avoid values that do not print (pure 00FF00 greens, pure magenta). Lines thinner than 2 px at 300 dpi will disappear.

**Do not include** any text, logos or the words Calle Nueve; I set the type. No real hotel names, no trademarked characters, no traced stock or AI output.
