## Fiverr post (copy from here)

**Title:** Miami illustration for a souvenir card deck (card back + box). Fun, colorful, your take on Miami.

**Category:** Graphics & Design > Illustration

**Description:**

I make Calle Nueve, a Cuban domino game printed as a deck of cards (callenueve.com). I am doing a Miami souvenir edition for the gift shops on Calle Ocho and South Beach, and I need original art for the back of the card and the front of the box.

**The brief in one line:** draw Miami. Your Miami. The version a tourist wants to take home.

I am not looking for a specific style. I want fun colors and fun vibes: the feeling of the city, not a landmark checklist. Some things that say Miami to people: pink and teal, palms, flamingos, Art Deco hotels, the beach, cafecito, Little Havana, dominoes, neon, the ocean, the heat. Use what you want, skip what you do not. Surprise me.

**What I do not want:** photo-real, 3D renders, generic clip art, stock-looking vectors, AI-generated art, text or logos inside the art.

**Deliverables:**
1. Card back illustration, delivered at exactly 597 x 1122 px, 300 dpi. Players see the backs of every card on the table, so it should look right when the card is upside down too: either symmetric top to bottom, or a centered subject that does not care which way is up. Keep anything important 72 px away from the edges.
2. Box front illustration, 549 x 1070 px at 300 dpi. Same art cropped, or a companion piece. Leave a clear band for the title (I add the text).
3. One square spot illustration (a single element from your design, like the flamingo or the palm) on a transparent background, 1200 x 1200 px PNG, for the box back.
4. Layered source file (PSD, or AI/SVG) plus flattened PNGs. Full commercial rights, all uses, worldwide, no attribution. Please price that in.

**Colors:** your call. If it helps, my brand uses teal 0D9488, pink F472B6, yellow EAB308 and cream F6EFDF, but the deck does not have to.

**Process:** one rough sketch for approval, one color comp, then final. I answer within a day. When you reply, send two or three pieces of yours that feel like this: colorful, fun, a place or a mood. That is what I choose on.

**Timeline:** two weeks.

**Budget:** [set it: $150 to $400 is the going range for this scope with full rights]

## Spec sheet to send after hiring

**Card back.** Canvas 597 x 1122 px, 300 dpi, RGB (sRGB), PNG plus the layered source. The outer 36 px on every side is trimmed off (bleed); art must run to the edge. Keep anything you care about inside the 72 px safe zone (a 453 x 978 px window in the middle). The back must look right upside down: symmetric top to bottom, or a centered subject. A one-way picture is acceptable only if we agree on it first.

**Box front.** 549 x 1070 px at 300 dpi. The whole panel is visible; keep 30 px clear of every edge. Reserve a band about 549 x 200 px (top or middle) as a flat area or plain cartouche where I place the title and a subtitle.

**Spot illustration.** 1200 x 1200 px, transparent PNG plus the vector or layered source. Single subject, no background, must read at 1 in and at 0.4 in.

**Color.** RGB is fine; the printer converts. Avoid values that do not print (pure 00FF00 greens, pure magenta). Lines thinner than 2 px at 300 dpi will disappear.

**Do not include** any text, logos or the words Calle Nueve; I set the type. No real hotel names, no trademarked characters, no traced stock or AI output.
