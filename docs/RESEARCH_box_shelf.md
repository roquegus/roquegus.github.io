# Box shelf research and plan (2026-09-24)

Full page with mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/VAed5UBCxga9QpCAMAe7K5 (private to Gus).

Findings:
- Shoppers glance about three seconds. Front panel: one main image, no more than three callouts. (ECRM, Maadho)
- Game boxes carry player count, age, play time and a one-line description on the front.
- Our look-alike is the Miami souvenir poker deck (same tuck box shape, same shops). The box must not read as poker cards.
- Domino-card competitors (Bicycle Double 9 Dominoes, Regal Games, NewVenture) put "Dominoes" in the name and pips on the front. None are Cuban or Miami.
- Souvenir shops sell small items from counter trays, spinners and peg hooks by the register.
- Independent shops rarely need a UPC; chains and Amazon do. Leave a barcode spot on the back.
- US: a product labeled for ages 12 or under is a children's product (CPSIA third-party testing). Recommend "13+".

Proposed rules (awaiting Gus): "DOMINOES" is the biggest word on front, spine and lid; deck name second; brand small at top; a domino card on the front; four fact boxes (2-4 players, 13+, 20 min, 55 cards); one Spanish line; three-step how-to-play and QR on the back; keep one flat color. Retail: counter tray of 12 with a header card, one open demo deck per shop, optional "Dominoes!" seal sticker, ask printers about a window box in domino size.
